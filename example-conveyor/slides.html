The whole device

One belt, one sensor, one arm

A DC gear motor turns the drive roller and the belt carries the cans at 8 cm/s, one can every 1.5 s. A colour sensor above the belt reads each can as it passes.

A servo arm pushes the red cans into the side bin. Every other can rides on and drops into the bin at the end of the belt.

Station 1 · Reading

The colour sensor reads every can

The sensor hangs from a bridge about 2 cm above the cans. Two white LEDs light the can below, and the chip measures how much red, green and blue light comes back.

The controller compares the three values. When red is clearly the strongest, it marks the can as red and starts a timer for the arm.

Station 2 · Sorting

The servo arm pushes red cans aside

The arm waits 12 cm after the sensor. At 8 cm/s a can needs 12 ÷ 8 = 1.5 s to arrive, so the controller waits that long and then swings the servo.

The arm sweeps about 70° across the belt, slides the can over the edge into the red bin, and returns before the next can comes.
